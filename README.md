📌 Project Overview

Prism Insurance Analysis is a data analytics project developed as part of my learning journey through the Complete Data Analyst Bootcamp: From Basic to Advanced on Udemy.

The project focuses on analyzing insurance data to understand premium performance, policy coverage, claim amounts, claim status, and customer demographics.

Using Microsoft SQL Server for data retrieval and Microsoft Power BI for visualization, this project transforms raw data into actionable business insights through an interactive dashboard.

🔍 Key Analysis Performed

- Premium Analysis: Evaluated premium contributions across different insurance policy types.
- Policy Type Analysis: Compared Travel, Health, Auto, Life, and Home insurance.
- Claim Status Analysis: Examined Settled, Pending, and Rejected claims.
- Customer Demographics: Explored customer distribution by age and gender.
- Data Quality Analysis: Identified missing claim dates and zero claim amounts associated with rejected claims.
- Business Recommendations: Identified opportunities to improve claim monitoring and reporting reliability.

💡 Key Insights

- ✈️ Travel Insurance contributed the highest premium among the policy types analyzed.
- 📊 Claim status analysis highlighted the importance of monitoring pending claims and understanding rejection reasons.
- 🔎 Data quality checks identified records requiring careful interpretation before drawing business conclusions.
- 🎯 Customer demographic analysis provided additional perspectives for understanding the insurance portfolio.

🎯 Business Recommendations

1. Monitor Pending Claims: Track pending claims by age and establish clear processing targets.
2. Analyze Rejected Claims: Capture rejection reasons to identify recurring issues and potential process improvements.
3. Track Policy Performance: Monitor premium and claim trends across different policy types.
4. Improve Data Quality: Validate missing dates, zero claim amounts, duplicate records, and key fields.
5. Expand KPI Monitoring: Consider adding claim settlement rate, rejection rate, pending claim ageing, and average claim amount.
