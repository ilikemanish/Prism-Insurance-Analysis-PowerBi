# 🚀 PRISM INSURANCE ANALYSIS
> **📊 Transforming Insurance Data into Actionable Business Insights**
## 💎 Project Overview
**Prism Insurance Analysis** is an end-to-end data analytics project developed as part of my learning journey through the *Complete Data Analyst Bootcamp: From Basic to Advanced* on Udemy.
The project explores insurance premiums, policy coverage, claims, and customer demographics to uncover meaningful patterns and support data-driven decision-making. 
Using Microsoft SQL Server for data retrieval and Microsoft Power BI for interactive visualization, this project demonstrates how raw insurance data can be transformed into valuable business insights.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze premium contributions across insurance policy types.
- Evaluate claim status and identify monitoring opportunities.
- Understand customer distribution by age and gender.
- Develop meaningful KPIs for insurance performance.
- Identify data quality issues and recommend improvements.
- Present findings through a clear, interactive dashboard.
---
## 🔍 Analysis Highlights
- 💰 **Premium Analysis:** Compare premium contributions across Travel, Health, Auto, Life, and Home insurance.
- 📋 **Claims Analysis:** Explore Settled, Pending, and Rejected claims to understand claim status distribution.
- 👥 **Customer Analytics:** Analyze customer demographics by age and gender.
- 🧹 **Data Quality:** Identify missing claim dates, zero claim amounts, and records requiring validation.
---
## 💡 Key Insights
1. ✈️ **Travel insurance** contributed the highest premium among the policy types analyzed.
2. 📊 **Claim status analysis** helps highlight where pending claims and rejection reasons need further investigation.
3. 🧹 **Data quality checks** are important for ensuring reliable insurance reporting.
4. 🎯 **Customer demographic analysis** provides additional context for understanding the insurance portfolio.
*Note: Figures are in dataset source units; currency was not explicitly specified.*

## 🚀 Business Recommendations
- **01 — Improve Claim Monitoring:** Track pending claims by ageing category and establish processing targets.
- **02 — Investigate Claim Rejections:** Record rejection reasons and identify recurring patterns that may require process improvements.
- **03 — Monitor Policy Performance:** Compare premium contributions and claim trends across policy types.
- **04 — Strengthen Data Quality:** Validate missing dates, zero claim amounts, duplicate records, and key identifiers before reporting.
- **05 — Expand the KPI Framework:** Consider adding claim settlement rate, rejection rate, pending claim ageing, and average claim amount.
